# Separate exact control-design exploration

This protocol is saved after the frozen main study's weak result and before this scan. It is **exploratory model-based design**, not a prespecified confirmation of the original setting. The main records, thresholds, source and fourteen hardware circuits remain unchanged. No empirical dot data or main held-out records enter the scan.

## Question and allowed family

Can a specified source-frequency shift and gap rotation improve distinguishability of the existing coherent-gap and fully dephased-gap hypotheses, at a declared acquisition resource? Keep delta/Gamma=1, lifetime1ns, eta=.8, e01=.01, e10=.02 and optical mode overlap1. The environment starts I/2. Evaluate the full distribution of four consecutive attempted pair observations (three possible outcomes each), retaining its conditional state between all four pairs.

Scan gap angles j*pi/16 for j=0..16, plus the original pi/3. Scan nonnegative second-emission shift amplitudes a/Gamma in [0,.25,.5,.75,1,1.5,2,3,4]. Four fixed patterns are [a,a,a,a], [a,-a,a,-a], [0,a,-a,0], and [a,0,-a,0]. All648 combinations are retained, including physical duplicate settings. No angle/shift optimization outside this grid is performed. One source shift changes the optical wavepacket through the existing complex Gram matrix; it does not set a final detection probability.

Primary design score is the exact Bhattacharyya information per attempted pair, -log(sum_y sqrt(P_Q(y) P_C(y)))/4. Ties are resolved by fixed enumeration order. Also save both directional KL divergences, total variation, exact log-likelihood-ratio moments and single-pair marginals. These are distinguishability of two specified mathematical laws, not unique identification of a quantum environment among arbitrary classical processes.

## Resource and power calculation

Power calculations concatenate **independent four-pair blocks**, with environment reset to I/2 before every block. They do not reuse a retained state across blocks. That reset is an additional ideal preparation assumption; its physical time, fidelity, control energy and optical compatibility are not known. Each block has four attempted photon pairs/eight source excitations and an expected2.56 usable pairs at eta=.8. All power resources count attempted pairs, including lost pairs. No wall-clock time is inferred from the radiative lifetime alone.

For independent blocks let L=log(P_Q/P_C), B=sum sqrt(P_Q P_C), alpha=.05 and beta=.20. With threshold log(beta/alpha), Markov's inequality gives P_C(sum L >= threshold)<=sqrt(alpha/beta)*B^n and P_Q(sum L < threshold)<=sqrt(beta/alpha)*B^n. Thus ceil(log(sqrt(alpha*beta))/log(B)) blocks is a rigorous **sufficient**, possibly conservative, bound for false flags<=5% and power>=80%, under the exact nominal simple hypotheses and independent perfect resets. It is not a necessary budget or a robust calibration guarantee. A separate normal approximation uses exact L moments and normal quantiles; it is explicitly an approximation and not a guarantee.

Re-evaluate the selected design and original four-pair prefix at eta1,.8,.5,.2 (holding readout confusion fixed) to expose loss cost. A separate ideal detector endpoint eta1/e01=e10=0 is labeled ideal. Retain whole four-pair distributions and exact effects. No synthetic holdout is generated by this stage.
