Rewind & Amplify: Grover's algorithm inside a quantum recurrent state machine

1. A rare event in a 6-step run of the hidden-defect machine
   Question: what do the runs with a click at step 6 look like? Only 8.0% of runs have one.
   The reset is gone: each step gets a fresh light, so the 6-step run is one reversible circuit A.

   1000 shots each, on Aer. Usable shots are the ones with the click:

   plain sampling               ███·································   7.8%   distance from the exact answer 0.077
   steer + 1 Grover iteration   ████████████████████████████████████ 100.0%   distance from the exact answer 0.010
   steer + local reflection     ███████████████████████████████████·  96.8%   distance from the exact answer 0.054

   Steering tuned the last emission from γ = 0.2 to 0.623, so p' = 1/4 and one iteration is enough.
   That scales every matching history by the same factor, so the answer stays exact.
   Most likely runs with that click: 000001 (89%), 000101 (3%), 000011 (3%)

2. Grover inside the loop: what happens right after the click?
   Amplify the click at step 3, then keep running the machine on fresh lights.

   With no hidden defect, a click wipes the machine, so what follows just restarts the run.
   A hidden defect remembers across the click. That difference is a fingerprint of hidden memory.

                P(click 1, 2, 3 steps later | click)       usable shots
   no defect    amplified 0.016  0.065  0.096   7,838 of 8,000
                exact     0.017  0.058  0.101
   one defect   amplified 0.011  0.027  0.045   7,669 of 8,000
                exact     0.010  0.030  0.047

3. The reader in the oracle slot: search an unsorted catalogue from a vague request
   Request: "how much did we sell in each area"
   Classical step, tokenising: ['sales', 'region']
   The reader reads 2 token(s), turning the phase of each of the 16 entries that contains them.

   After 3 Grover iterations, from 1000 shots on Aer:

   quarterly_sales_by_region          ████████████████████████······  81.4%   keyword score 1.00
   customer_churn_by_region           ██····························   6.0%   keyword score 0.50
   shipping_costs_by_region           ██····························   5.4%   keyword score 0.50
   monthly_sales_by_product           █·····························   4.7%   keyword score 0.50

   Every entry started at 6.2%. Partial matches get a partial boost: that is the vague part.

The honest part
   At this size a classical program answers all of this in about a millisecond, and exactly.
   The classical cost grew about 6 times per hidden qubit in our tests; these circuits grow slowly with it.
   So this shows an architecture that could matter for large hidden memories on future hardware,
   not a speedup today. The keyword search compiles its oracle from the catalogue, so it has no speedup at all
   on arbitrary data. See docs/REPORT.md.
