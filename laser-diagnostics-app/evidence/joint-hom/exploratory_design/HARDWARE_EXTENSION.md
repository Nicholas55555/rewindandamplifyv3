# Selected-design four-pair digital extension

This separate bundle was requested after the exact design and prospective optical-acquisition simulation. It does not change the prior fourteen-circuit bundle. It uses the selected theta=3*pi/4, control+2*Gamma and two specified gap models; four-pair passive controls at the same angle are included.

The builder reuses the validated photon-mode isometry from the parent module, retains the environment and all photon/bath registers through four pairs, and measures all qubits at the end. Four fresh pair-symmetry flags are obtained by H-controlledSWAP-H. The initial memory I/2 is purified by one qubit. Complete gap dephasing uses a new unobserved bath qubit after each of the seven rotations. Thus controlled coherent/dephased circuits use22/29qubits; passive circuits use14/21. These are digital representations of the optical observable, not measured photons or fabricated dots.

Every row declares `reference_marginal_qubits=[flag0,flag1,flag2,flag3,memory0]`. The corresponding NPZ vector has32entries with the first listed qubit as least significant bit. It is a marginal, **not a full22/29-qubit distribution**. Raw hardware strings retain all qubits in the one `meas` register. Memory is measured only in Z; a full conditional memory density matrix is an ideal reference, not experimentally reconstructed tomography from Z counts.

Aer matrix-product-state simulation verifies each32-dimensional flag/memory marginal and all16conditional2x2memory branches against direct CP-map propagation. Set truncation threshold1e-16 and no maximum-bond cap; report actual errors, runtime and simulator metadata. No full2^29statevector is allocated. Limit a local attempt to600seconds/4GiB. Check the new builder on one-pair and two-pair instances against the original<=15qubit circuit. Generic u/cx synthesis and QPY readback are validated before handoff.

The circuit assumes ideal complete emission, calibrated control and no optical loss/readout confusion. Physical QPU gate/readout errors are a different layer. Repeating a four-pair digital circuit is not replication of a multi-thousand-pair optical classifier, detector-calibration study, perfect physical environmental reset, or semiconductor experiment. No IBM submission is performed here; the parent campaign runner owns submission, resource decisions and observed-data analysis.
