# Attribution for imported reference data

**Dataset:** *Dataset for “Fast and efficient demultiplexing of single photons from a GaAs quantum dot with resonantly enhanced electro-optic modulators”* (2022).

**Creators:** Julian Münzberg; Franz Draxl; Saimon Filipe Covre da Silva; Yusuf Karli; Santanu Manna; Armando Rastelli; Gregor Weihs; Robert Keil.

**Source:** [Zenodo record 6337697](https://zenodo.org/records/6337697), DOI [10.5281/zenodo.6337697](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6337697).

**License:** [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The saved `zenodo_record.json` declares `metadata.license.id = "cc-by-4.0"`. This notice provides attribution and a license link; it does not replace the license's legal terms.

**Files covered:** the six original `HOM_QD5_*.csv` files listed in `provenance_manifest.json` and the original dataset `readme.md`, saved here as `source_readme.md`. Their bytes are unchanged and verified against the record's MD5 checksums. They retain this data license rather than being relicensed under the project's code license.

**Project changes:** `import_verify.py`, its tests, this documentation and the raw-count plots are project additions. The plot converts the time unit from ps to ns and shows a central display window; no counts, exposure normalization or visibility fit are added. The experimental authors did not produce or endorse this audit, the QRSM research or a lithography application.

**Related paper:** [Münzberg et al., arXiv:2203.08682v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.08682v1). Paper-reported corrected visibilities are explicitly labeled as external published results; they are not recomputed measurements.
